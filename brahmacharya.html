🌊 Urge Surfing
The urge will peak, and it will pass. You are the observer.
STEP 1
Notice the urge. Name it: "I feel an urge."
STEP 2
Observe where it lives in the body. Chest? Throat? Belly? Don't fight it.
STEP 3
Breathe slowly into that place. Watch it rise — it always peaks, always passes.
STEP 4
The urge is not you. You are the observer. Redirect: cold water, walking, push-ups, call someone.
